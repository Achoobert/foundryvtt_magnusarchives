## Example Characters

- **Source document:** *The Magnus Archives Roleplaying Game* (Rusty Quill Ltd., 2024), pages 379-380 — the filled-in sample character sheet.
- **Applicable license:** none claimed. The Magnus Archives is Rusty Quill's intellectual property and is **not** covered by the Cypher Open License. The Cypher System framing (Pools, Edge, Effort, tier, the damage track) is CRD / COL material.
- **Source section:** the printed sample sheet's identity line, stat Pools, skills, special abilities, cyphers, attacks, equipment, background, connections and character arc.
- **Copied, adapted, or original:** adapted. Every block of prose on the sheet is paraphrased, not transcribed. Skill, equipment and weapon entries reuse the already-reviewed system Compendium documents by reference rather than restating them. The Investigator Type and the Explores Dark Places Focus are placeholder Items carrying only what the sample sheet itself shows: the Type records its light/medium weapon practice, and the Focus a two-node tier-1 graph for the abilities the sheet lists. Neither reproduces its full rules text.
- **Author of the adaptation:** project owner, AI-assisted.
- **Date added / last reviewed:** 2026-10-26.
- **Open question:** Rusty Quill's redistribution terms for sample-character material are unresolved. Confirm before any public release.
